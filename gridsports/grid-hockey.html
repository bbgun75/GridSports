Rules

Learn It in
One Round.

01

Pick a Team

Each player picks a team card. Tier A teams have more GOAL cells — they shoot more and score more. Tier D teams have fewer. Someone always picks Buffalo on a dare.

02

Roll Your Shots

Roll 1d6 and add your card's shot bonus for your total shots on goal. Alternate shots with your opponent — red die for column, blue die for row.

03

Check the Result

GOAL means it's in. SAVE, BLOCK, POST, or WIDE means it isn't. Mark your goals and keep rolling until both players are out of shots.

04

Most Goals Wins

Count your goals. Most goals wins 2 pts. Tied? Flip a coin — OT winner gets 1 pt. Loser gets nothing and has to live with it.

FAQ

Questions.

How do you play Grid Hockey?+
Each player picks a team card. Roll 1d6 and add your card's shot bonus for your total shots on goal. Alternate shots — red die for column, blue die for row. GOAL means it's in. SAVE, BLOCK, POST, or WIDE means it isn't. Most goals wins 2 pts. Tied? Flip a coin — OT winner gets 1 pt. Loser gets nothing and has to live with it.
Do I need a phone or app to play?+
No. Grid Hockey is a completely physical pen-and-paper game. No app, no screen, no battery. Just the notebook and two dice.
Are the teams based on real data?+
Yes. Every team card is built from real 2025–26 standings and scoring data. Tier A teams have more GOAL cells. Tier D teams have fewer. The data shows up on the grid — it's not just cosmetic.
How long does a game take?+
About 10–15 minutes. You can run a single game, a full playoff bracket, or an entire season on paper. It scales to however much time you have.
Where can I buy Grid Hockey?+
Grid Hockey is available on Amazon through the Grid Sports store. Ships to you as a physical notebook — just add dice.
Can I play solo or do I need two players?+
You can play solo — pick both teams and run the shots back to back. Works great as a solo sim game. Also plays well head-to-head or in a tournament bracket format with more players.
