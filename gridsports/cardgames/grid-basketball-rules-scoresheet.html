01
What You Need
Two standard six-sided dice in different colours — one acts as the Red Die (column), the other as the Blue Die (row).

Pull the cards for the two teams you want to play. Place them side by side.

You'll need a pencil and this score sheet, or the roll tracker printed on each card.
02
How to Play
Pick a team to play first. Roll both dice. The Red Die gives the column (1–6), the Blue Die gives the row (1–6). Find that cell on the card — the number is the points scored on that possession run.

Record the points. Roll again. Repeat until you have made 12 rolls. Add all 12 point values together for the team's final score.

Then play the second team's card the same way. 12 rolls, add them up.
03
Winning & Tiebreaker
The team with the higher final score wins the game. Record the result on your score sheet.

Tiebreaker: If both teams finish on the exact same score, each player rolls one die. Higher single die roll wins — this is Sudden Death Overtime. Re-roll again if tied a second time.

Record OT wins separately on your score sheet with a ★ mark.
04
The 🔥 Wildcard Cell
Tier 3, 4 and 5 teams have two wildcard cells marked with 🔥 and a value of 13. These represent a hot shooting stretch — the kind of run that keeps a struggling team in a game.

Wildcard cells work exactly like normal cells — just record the 13 points. No special action required. Their rarity is what makes them special.
05
Season Play
Use the score sheet to run a full season. Pick any matchups you like — play every team against every other, run a conference schedule, or simulate the playoffs bracket.

Track wins, losses, and final scores across the sheet. At season end, tally each team's record to crown a champion.

Tip: Play all 30 teams round-robin over a weekend for a full simulated season.
06
Reading the Cards
Each card shows the team's real 2025–26 ORTG (offensive rating per 100 possessions) and their offensive rank in the league. This determines which tier they belong to.

The footer badge shows the tier: Elite, Strong, Average, Below Average, or Rebuilding. Better teams have higher cell values and a higher scoring floor — but any team can have a big night.
