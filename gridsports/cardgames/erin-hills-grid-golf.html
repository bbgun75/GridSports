⛳ ERIN HILLS — GRID GOLF

18 individual hole cards · Roll 2d6 each shot · Die 1 = Row · Die 2 = Column · Wind penalty applies when marked!

HOW TO PLAY — 2 ROLLS PER HOLE:
Roll 1 — TEE SHOT: Sets your position. Fairway/Rough = no penalty. Fescue/Sand = +1. Water/OB = +2. Birdie Zone = −1.
Roll 2 — APPROACH: Seals the score. Rough/Fescue/Sand = +1. Water/OB = +2. Birdie = −1. Eagle = −2.
WIND RULE (optional): Before each hole, roll 1d6. On a 5 or 6, wind sweeps across the open prairie — all penalties increase by +1 this hole.

Add both adjustments to Par for your final score. Write it on the card!

EXAMPLE: Par 4 · Tee = Fescue (+1) · Approach = Birdie Zone (−1) → Score = 4 (Par)
EXAMPLE: Par 3 · Tee = OB (+2) · Approach = Fairway (0) → Score = 5 — DOUBLE BOGEY
