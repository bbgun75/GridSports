How To Play

A pen & paper pro wrestling dice game
What You Need

Two wrestler cards. Two six-sided dice. A pencil. That's it. No app, no timer, no rulebook. Everything you need is on the card.

How To Play
1
Each player picks a wrestler card. Place them face up in front of you.
2
Players alternate turns. On your turn, roll both dice. The first die is the column. The second die is the row.
3
Find where the column and row meet on your grid. Follow the result.
4
Keep rolling until someone lands a PIN with a full momentum meter.
Results
PIN If your momentum meter is full, you win. If not, the ref waves it off and nothing happens.
HIT Fill in one momentum box on your card. Get 5 to unlock your PIN.
STUN Your opponent skips their next turn. The crowd goes wild.
BLOCK Your opponent's next HIT won't count. No momentum gained from it.
GRAPPLE Nothing happens. You're locked up. The crowd starts a slow clap.
Momentum Meter

Every HIT fills one box. Fill all 5 and your PIN cell is live. Land a PIN before the meter is full and the ref ignores it. Both wrestlers have their own meter — you can lose the lead fast.

Every card has a unique grid. The same roll hits differently depending on who you picked.
Tiers

Each wrestler belongs to a tier. Tiers change the grid — not the rules. A Jobber can beat a Main Eventer. It just doesn't happen often.

Tier HITs GRAPPLEs PINs Vibe
JOBBER 10 17 2 Grinds. Struggles. Somehow still tries.
MID-CARD 14 12 2 Balanced. Could go either way.
MAIN EVENT 17 8 3 Loaded. Fast. Here to end you.
Tips

HANDICAP MATCH — Put a Jobber against a Main Eventer if one player needs a head start. The Jobber will probably lose. Probably.

FULL CARD — Run 4-6 matches in a row, mixing tiers. Crown a winner at the end.

TOURNAMENT — Single elimination. 8 wrestlers, 3 rounds. Bracket it on paper.
