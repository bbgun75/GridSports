⛳ AUGUSTA NATIONAL — GRID GOLF

18 individual hole cards · Roll 2d6 each shot · Die 1 = Row · Die 2 = Column

HOW TO PLAY — 2 ROLLS PER HOLE:
Roll 1 — TEE SHOT: Sets your position. Fairway/Rough = no penalty. Sand/Water/OB = +1. Birdie Zone = −1.
Roll 2 — APPROACH: Seals the score. Rough/Sand/Trees = +1. Water/OB = +2. Birdie = −1. Eagle = −2.

Add both adjustments to Par for your final score. Write it on the card!

EXAMPLE: Par 4 · Tee = Rough (0) · Approach = Birdie Zone (−1) → Score = 3 🐦
EXAMPLE: Par 3 · Tee = OB (+1) · Approach = Fairway (0) → Score = 4 + BOGEY
