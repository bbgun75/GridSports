Rules

Learn It in
One Round.

01

Pick Two Cards

Each player picks a team card. All 32 teams are included, each built from real 2025–26 scoring offense data. Better offenses have more TD cells. Struggling offenses have more punts and the occasional fumble.

02

Run 12 Drives

Each team runs 12 offensive drives. Roll both dice — red die is the row, blue die is the column. Find the cell and read the result. TD scores 7. FG scores 3. First down means roll again, the drive keeps going. Punt or turnover ends it.

03

Go For It

On any FG cell, you can gamble and go for the TD instead. Roll one die — a 1 converts. Anything else is a turnover on downs. It's your call every single time. Las Vegas goes for it a lot. This tracks.

04

Most Points Wins

After 12 drives each, highest score wins. Tied? Each team gets 3 overtime drives until someone pulls ahead. Alternate drives or run both teams back to back — either way works.

FAQ

Questions.

How do you play Grid Football? +
Each player picks a team card. Each team runs 12 drives — roll both dice (red = row, blue = column), find the cell. TD scores 7, FG scores 3, first downs extend the drive, punts and turnovers end it. On any FG, you can go for it by rolling a single die — 1 converts, anything else is a turnover. Most points after 12 drives wins. Tied? Overtime drives until someone scores more.
Do I need a phone or app to play? +
No. Grid Football is a completely physical pen-and-paper game. No app, no screen, no battery, no wi-fi. The notebook is the game. All you add are two dice — one red, one blue.
Are the teams based on real data? +
Yes. Every team card is built from real 2025–26 scoring offense data. Better offenses have more TD cells. Weaker offenses have more punts. Each card also shows PPG, offense grade, and defense grade. The data shows up on the grid — it's not just cosmetic.
How long does a game take? +
About 10–15 minutes for a full game. 12 drives each, resolves fast. You can run a quick single game, a full playoff bracket, or an entire season on paper. It scales to however much time you have.
Where can I buy Grid Football? +
Grid Football is available on Amazon through the Grid Sports store. Ships to you as a physical notebook — just add dice.
Can I play solo or do I need two players? +
You can play solo — pick both teams and run the drives back to back. Works great as a solo sim game. Also plays well head-to-head or in a tournament bracket format with more players.
