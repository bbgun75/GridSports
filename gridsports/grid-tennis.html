Rules

Learn It in
One Game.

01

Pick a Player

Each player picks a card from the roster of 40. Cards are color-coded by preferred surface — clay, grass, or hard. Every grid reflects the full player: serve, return, consistency, aggression. Djokovic outlasts. Bublik explodes. Paolini never misses.

02

Roll Simultaneously

Both players roll two dice at the same time — red die for column, blue die for row. Find your cell: Green, White, or Red. Green beats White and Red. White beats Red. Tie goes to the server. That's one game.

03

Switch and Repeat

Switch server and roll again. First to six games wins the set — lead by two. At 6–6, one tiebreak roll each. Same rules. Server wins ties. Best of three sets wins the match.

04

Play the Surface

Pick your surface before the match. Clay specialists start every set 1–0 on their home dirt. Grass and hard court specialists get the same on theirs. If both players share the same surface, the bonus cancels out. Pick the matchup carefully.

FAQ

Questions.

How do you play Grid Tennis?+
Each player picks a card. Both roll two dice simultaneously — red die for column, blue die for row. Find your cell: Green, White, or Red. Green beats White and Red. White beats Red. Tie goes to the server. Switch server every game. First to six wins the set. Best of three sets wins the match.
What does the surface bonus do?+
Surface specialists start every set 1–0 when playing on their preferred surface — clay, grass, or hard. If both players share the same surface specialty, the bonus cancels out. Declare the surface before the match starts.
Do I need a phone or app to play?+
No. Grid Tennis is a completely physical pen-and-paper game. No app, no screen, no battery. Just the notebook and two dice.
Are the grids based on real player data?+
Yes. Every card reflects the full player — serve, return, consistency, and aggression — weighted by real match results. The grid distribution shows how a player actually wins or loses games, not just how they serve.
How long does a match take?+
About five minutes for a best-of-three match. You can run a quick single set, a full match, or bracket out an entire tournament on paper.
Can I play solo?+
Yes. Pick both players and roll for each. Works great as a solo sim — run a full draw and see who wins the tournament.
Where can I buy Grid Tennis?+
Grid Tennis is available on Amazon through the Grid Sports author store in both Men's (ATP) and Women's (WTA) editions.
