Rules

Learn It in
One Race.

01

Pick Your Horse

Choose any horse from the field of 30 before the gate opens — no changing your mind once you've picked. Each horse has a trait: Speed, Stamina, Turf, or Mud. Check the track card to see which trait gets a boost and which gets a penalty this race.

02

Run the Whole Field

For each horse: red die for column, blue die for row. Find the cell — SURGE, STRONG, STEADY, FADE, STALL, or STUMBLE. Each has a value. Apply any +1 or -1 track modifier based on the horse's trait. Roll 3 times and add for their race total.

03

Rank the Field

After rolling all 30 horses, rank by total — highest wins. Ties go to better odds. Mark finishing positions on the track card. The favorite doesn't always win. The 40-1 shot occasionally does something embarrassing to everyone's expectations.

04

Check Your Pick

Win, Place, Show, or out of the money. Run all 8 tracks to complete the full Triple Crown + Championship Circuit season. Track your picks on the season tally — most wins across the circuit takes the title.

The Circuit

Eight Tracks.
Eight Different Races.

Every track boosts one horse trait and punishes another. Pick accordingly — or find out the hard way what happens to a Speed horse at Belmont.

30 horses.
8 tracks.
One pick.

Every horse in Grid Horse Racing has a trait — Speed, Stamina, Turf, or Mud — and a tier that determines how their 6×6 grid is weighted. Tier 1 favorites have more SURGE and STRONG cells. Tier 4 longshots have more STALL and STUMBLE cells, with rare explosions of brilliance.

Each track modifies every roll for the right trait by +1, and every roll for the wrong trait by -1. Over 3 rolls, that's a swing of up to 6 points — enough to move a horse from the back of the field into contention, or drop a favorite out of the money.

The notebook ships to your door. Two dice (one red, one blue) are all you add.

FAQ

Questions.

How do you play Grid Horse Racing?+
Pick one horse from the field of 30 before rolling — no changing your mind. For each horse, roll both dice three times: red die for column, blue die for row. Find the cell value (SURGE=8, STRONG=7, STEADY=6, FADE=5, STALL=4, STUMBLE=2). Apply any +1 or -1 track modifier based on the horse's trait. Add all three values for that horse's race total. Do this for all 30 horses, rank by total — highest wins. Ties go to better odds.
What's in the notebook?+
30 horse cards each with a full 6×6 grid, 8 real track cards, an instruction card, and a season tally. All you need to add are two dice — one red, one blue.
Do I need a phone or app?+
No. Grid Horse Racing is a completely physical pen-and-paper game. No app, no screen, no battery. Just the notebook and two dice.
How do track modifiers work?+
Every track boosts one horse trait (+1 per roll) and penalizes another (-1 per roll). Louisville boosts Stamina and hurts Turf horses. Del Mar favors Speed and punishes Stamina. Gulfstream is turf country — Turf horses get the boost, Mud horses are useless. Each horse card shows their trait. Each track card shows which traits are boosted and penalized, with a short explanation of why.
How long does a full race take?+
About 5 minutes for a single race. Running the full 8-track season takes 30–40 minutes total, or you can spread it across sessions — each track card is self-contained.
Can I play solo?+
Yes. Pick your horse, roll the whole field yourself. Works as a solo sim. Also plays well with multiple people — each player picks a horse before rolling, and you run the whole field once for everyone.
Where can I buy it?+
Grid Horse Racing is available on Amazon through the Grid Sports author store. Ships as a physical notebook — just add dice.
