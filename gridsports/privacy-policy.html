Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 7, 2026

Golf Card does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The game runs entirely on your device and does not require an internet connection or user account.

🔒 No Data Collection

Golf Card is designed with your privacy as the default. We do not collect, transmit, or store any information about you or your use of the app — ever.

  • No personal information collected
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No crash reporting sent to external servers
  • No advertising identifiers used

📵 Offline Operation

Golf Card works entirely offline. The app requires no internet connection to function, and no data is ever sent to or received from any remote server. Your scores, sessions, and gameplay remain solely on your device.

👤 No Account Required

You do not need to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details — including your name, email, or location — to use Golf Card. The app is ready to play the moment you open it.

🤝 No Third-Party Sharing

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to share. Golf Card does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or data-broker services.

👶 Children's Privacy

Golf Card does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for players of all ages.

📋 Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is ever updated, the revised version will be made available within the app and will reflect a new effective date. Continued use of Golf Card after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

✉️ Contact

Have a question about this privacy policy?

Reach out through the Golf Card app page on the App Store or Play Store, and we'll be happy to help. Since no personal data is collected, there is no user data to request, modify, or delete.
